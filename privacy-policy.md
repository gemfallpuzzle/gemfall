# Privacy Policy — Gemfall

**App:** Gemfall (Android package `com.puzzlygames.gemfall`)

**Developer:** `Bamke Haretuno`

**Contact:** `gemfallpuzzle@gmail.com`

**Last updated:** `2026/08/27`

Gemfall is a block puzzle game. There is no sign-up, no account and no login.
You never give us your name, email address, phone number or payment details,
and the game has no in-app purchases.

The game is free and supported by advertising, and it reports crashes and
anonymous usage statistics so it can be fixed and improved. This policy
explains exactly what that means, what leaves your device, and what stays
on it.

---

## 1. What stays on your device

Your progress and preferences are stored in the app's own local storage on
your phone and are **not** uploaded anywhere:

- the board, tray, score and combo of a game in progress
- your best score and your Adventure level progress and stars
- Sound and Vibration settings
- whether you have already seen the tutorial or a one-time in-game message
- a cached copy of the app's configuration (see section 4)

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Clearing the app's storage from
Android **Settings → Apps → Gemfall → Storage** does the same.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gemfall shows three kinds of ad: a full-screen ad when the app opens, a banner
at the bottom of the screen during play, and an optional rewarded video you may
choose to watch to continue a finished run. Rewarded video is always your
choice — nothing in the game requires you to watch one.

Ads are served by **Google AdMob**. To select and measure ads, Google and its
ad partners may collect:

- your device's **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier)
- device information such as model, operating system version, language and
  screen size
- your **approximate location**, derived from your IP address
- how you interacted with an ad (that it was shown, clicked, or that a rewarded
  video was completed), for fraud prevention and measurement

We do not receive your advertising ID or any profile built from it; we only
receive aggregate earnings reports. Google's handling of this data is described
in the **Google Privacy Policy** (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and in
**How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services**
(<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>).

**Your choice.** In regions where consent is required (the EEA, the UK and
Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form the first time it runs, and
your choice decides whether ads are personalised and whether analytics is
collected. You can change it later from the game's Settings, where available.
Everywhere, you can limit ad tracking on the device itself: **Settings →
Google → All services → Ads → Delete advertising ID** (wording varies by
Android version).

## 3. Analytics, crash reporting and notifications (Google Firebase)

- **Firebase Analytics** records anonymous gameplay events — for example that a
  level was started or finished — together with a randomly generated app
  instance identifier and basic device and country information. It tells us
  which levels are too hard and which features go unused. It is never linked to
  a name or an email address, because we hold neither. Where consent applies,
  analytics collection follows your consent choice.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** sends a diagnostic report if the game crashes or hits
  an error: the stack trace, the app version, the device model and the Android
  version. It contains no personal information.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** is used to deliver push notifications, if you
  allow them. This involves a device push token, which identifies your
  installation and nothing else. You can turn notifications off at any time in
  Android's app settings, and the game works exactly the same without them.

Firebase is operated by Google and is covered by the same Google privacy policy
linked above.

## 4. The app's configuration request

Once per launch, the app asks our server for its current settings — how often
ads appear, and whether there is an update notice or an announcement to show.
The request **sends no data about you or your game**. It is a plain read; the
reply is the same for every player.

Like any web server, ours records standard request information such as the IP
address and the time of the request in its logs. These logs are used only to
keep the service running and secure, and are kept for
`[NUMBER — e.g. 30]` days.

If the request fails, the game falls back to its last cached settings and
carries on. Nothing about your gameplay is ever sent to us.

## 5. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Internet / network state | Loading ads and the configuration request |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Required by Android 13+ to serve ads |
| Vibration | Haptic feedback on a clear, which you can turn off in Settings |
| Notifications | Only if you allow them, for announcements about the game |

Gemfall does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos,
files or precise location.

## 6. Children

Gemfall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the app, please contact us at the address above
and we will delete it.

## 7. Your rights and your data

Because we operate no accounts, we hold no profile of you that we could look
up. To exercise control over what is collected:

- **Reset or delete your advertising ID** in Android settings, as described in
  section 2, to break the link between your device and past ad activity.
- **Withdraw consent** in regions where the consent form applies.
- **Uninstall the app**, which removes everything stored on the device.
- **Write to us** at `[YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]` to ask what is held or to request
  deletion. We will respond within 30 days and will pass valid deletion
  requests on to Google where they concern data held by Firebase.

Data collected by Google is processed on Google's infrastructure, which may be
outside your country. Google's own privacy policy and its data-transfer
safeguards apply to it.

## 8. Security

Everything the app sends leaves the device over HTTPS. We hold no account
credentials and no payment data, so there is nothing of that kind to lose.
